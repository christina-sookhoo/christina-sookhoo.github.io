Lifecycle marketingApproximately 3× the performance of acquisition-only groups.

The situation

A home services client had focused its marketing on acquiring new customers.

The goal

I wanted to test whether campaigns for existing and inactive customers could uncover a new source of growth.

What I did

I proposed a test that focused on upselling existing customers and reactivating inactive ones. Our ongoing relationship gave the client confidence to try it.

Performance reporting & optimizationThree years of campaign results informed audience, timing, and messaging decisions.

The situation

Over three years with a home improvement client, I tracked total sales generated and campaign spend as a percentage of sales. Each sale was high value, so even a few additional customers could make a test worthwhile.

The goal

I wanted to use the reporting to decide where to put more of the budget and what to test next.

What I did

When one audience generated more sales than the others, we shifted a larger share of the campaign toward it and tested how sales held up further down selected ranked lists. I used seasonal patterns to adjust spend by month. To keep the testing simple for the client, we held the creative constant and A/B tested the messaging.

The result

We used the findings to change audience allocation and monthly spend. The A/B test identified which message performed better.

About Christina

A thoughtful approach
to work. And life.

I’m a Los Angeles–based marketing strategist and project manager with eight years of experience connecting client needs, campaign strategy, and delivery.

My background spans $15M+ in campaign portfolios across 20+ national brands. Earlier in my career, I supported marketing and operations across more than 60 franchise locations. That mix has shaped the way I work: understand the people involved, ask the right questions, and build a clear plan.

I’ve also been consulting on B2B marketing and account strategy for an equipment company, and developed an audience-growth roadmap for an independent music artist.

Outside work, I paint, crochet, explore new crafts, and make time for fitness and yoga. I’m certified in yin yoga, and my movement practice includes weight training, Pilates, and sculpt.

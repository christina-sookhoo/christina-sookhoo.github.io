The situation and goal

I worked with a retail client on direct mail for two years. We wanted to see whether adding channels would generate enough revenue to justify the investment.

Lifecycle marketingAbout 3× the response rate of acquisition-only groups.

The situation and goal

A home services client focused its marketing on acquiring new customers. I wanted to see if there was room to grow within its existing customer base.

What we did

I proposed a test that compared acquisition with offers for existing customers and campaigns to win back inactive customers.

The result

The retention and reactivation groups had about 3× the response rate of the acquisition-only groups. That gave the client a reason to include both audiences in future campaigns.

The challenge

Work was tracked inconsistently. Leadership lacked a shared view of priorities and progress, and the team needed a more consistent way to manage incoming work.

My approach

I built a tracking system in Excel and introduced weekly team meetings to establish shared priorities. As needs grew, I helped move the work into Smartsheet and later Asana, supporting adoption at each stage.

What changed

Leadership gained visibility into project status. The team had clearer priorities and a consistent process for taking on and tracking work.

The tools evolved. The goal stayed the same: give people a clear view of the work and what needed to happen next.

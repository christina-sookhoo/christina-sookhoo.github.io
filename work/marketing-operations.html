Workflow design, project tracking & team adoption

In this project

The situationThe goalWhat I didThe result

The situation

Teams tracked projects inconsistently, leaving leadership without a shared view of priorities or progress.

The goal

I wanted to give the team one way to take in work, set priorities, and track delivery.

What I did

I built a tracker in Excel and introduced weekly planning meetings. As the work grew, I helped move the process to Smartsheet and then Asana, supporting adoption at each step.

The result

Leadership could see project status, and the team had clearer priorities and a consistent way to manage incoming work.

The tools evolved. The goal stayed the same: give people a clear view of the work and what needed to happen next.

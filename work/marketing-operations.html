In this project

The situationThe goalWhat I didThe result

The situation

Teams tracked projects inconsistently, leaving leadership without a shared view of priorities or progress.

The goal

Leadership needed a single view of project status, and the team needed a consistent way to take in work, set priorities, and track delivery.

What I did

I started with Excel and weekly planning meetings to give the team a shared set of priorities. As the work grew, I helped move the process into Smartsheet and later Asana, supporting the team through each transition.

The result

~15 people used the tool to track 60+ projects at a time. The team missed fewer deadlines, and leadership had a clearer view of priorities and project status.

The tools evolved. The goal stayed the same: give people a clear view of the work and what needed to happen next.

The opportunity

A home-services client had focused on new-customer acquisition. Alongside that work, I saw an opportunity to engage people who were already in the customer base.

My approach

I introduced a test focused on upselling existing customers and reactivating inactive ones. The trust built through our ongoing work helped the client feel comfortable exploring the idea.

What changed

The new customer groups outperformed the acquisition groups in the test and opened a retention and reactivation dimension to the client’s marketing strategy.

I wanted the strategy to consider the full customer relationship, including what happens after acquisition.

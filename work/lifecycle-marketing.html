Lifecycle marketing

Growth beyond acquisition.

Finding opportunity in the customers a business already had.

My role
Audience strategy, client recommendations & campaign planning

In this project

The situationThe goalWhat I didThe result

The situation

A home services client had focused its marketing on acquiring new customers.

The goal

I wanted to test whether campaigns for existing and inactive customers could uncover a new source of growth.

What I did

I proposed a test that focused on upselling existing customers and reactivating inactive ones. Our ongoing relationship gave the client confidence to try it.

The result

The retention and reactivation groups performed approximately 3× as well as the acquisition-only groups. The result gave the client a reason to include both audiences in its strategy.

I wanted the strategy to consider the full customer relationship, including what happens after acquisition.

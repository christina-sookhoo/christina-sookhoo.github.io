A/B testing

Testing offers by customer tier

Using differences in customer response to shape a beauty brand’s campaigns.

My role
Identified differences in response across existing customer tiers

In this project

The situationThe goalWhat we testedWhat changed

The situation

I noticed that a beauty brand’s existing customers responded differently across the tiers we had identified.

The goal

We wanted to see whether tailoring messaging and offers by tier would increase sales.

What we tested

We tested different messages and offers across the tiers and compared sales with a control group.

What changed

The test produced an average 2.5× sales result versus the control group. We used the findings to tailor offers and messaging by tier in future campaigns.

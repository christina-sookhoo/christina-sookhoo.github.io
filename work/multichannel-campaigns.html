Expanding a direct mail campaign

A retail client tested whether digital and TV could build on two years of direct mail.

In this project

The situationThe goalWhat we testedWhat changed

The situation

I worked with a retail client on direct mail for two years.

The goal

We wanted to see whether adding channels would generate enough revenue to justify the investment.

What we tested

We added digital and TV advertising to direct mail and compared total campaign revenue with a control group.

What changed

My estimate is that the expanded campaign generated ~4× as much total campaign revenue as the control group. The client kept digital advertising in future campaigns, and we continued testing and refining the approach.

Client relationships

A long-standing account, restored.

Persistence, trust, and a coordinated response when it mattered.

My role
Client relationships, business development & internal coordination

In this project

The situationThe goalWhat I didThe result

The situation

A restaurant client changed agencies, disrupting our main point of contact and putting a 10-year relationship worth ~$2M in annual revenue at risk.

The goal

I needed to earn the new agency’s trust and protect the business we’d built over ten years.

What I did

I found the new agency and spent three months building credibility by walking them through our account history and data expertise. When another vendor failed, I coordinated our internal team and helped address the immediate need within a month.

The result

Within four months, we restored the relationship and kept the ~$2M annual account. Both the client and the new agency thanked us for the response.

Rebuilding the relationship meant finding the right people, staying consistent, and being ready to help.
